# Editorial review contract

`video-reference schema production` returns the default production mandates and `editorialQuality`. Reference `read` and Remotion handoffs carry the same versioned requirements. Project mandates and explicit author choices prevail. This command discovers requirements; it does not analyze speech, create screenshots, validate a storyboard or certify aesthetic quality.

When production is requested, the agent supplies a storyboard before assembly and updates it after edits, alongside the whole second-by-second REVIEW and complete editable Studio composition. Cover every chapter, selected milestone, CTA and speech-cut candidate. Panels need source/destination intervals, a timestamped real print with revision/provenance, actual speech or explicit absence/uncertainty, editorial purpose, action/reason, text/graphics/motion/audio anchors and editable controls. Label source, reference, historical and current-draft prints separately; preserve missing evidence honestly. Private media and real transcripts remain in the independent private project.

Choose milestones from the content: hook, discovery, attempt, setback, contrast, result, closing invitation. Explain their relevance, give them greater weight than ordinary speech and suppress competing elements. Make the author's CTA action/keyword unmistakable with readable holds and semantic anchors. Concrete sizes, palettes, fonts, layouts and optional glow are brand/reference choices; absent/not applicable CTA is valid when justified, and missing copy is proposed separately without inventing a promise.

Inspect the whole spoken source for mistakes, duplicate takes, false starts, redundant repetition and idle pauses. Keep a cut ledger with before/removed/after excerpts, exact source/destination clocks, action/reason, evidence and affected layers. Apply reversible cuts when they improve pace without changing meaning. Preserve intentional repetition, useful pauses, qualifiers and authentic failure admissions. No fixed number of cuts, mandatory ASR installation or separate transcript/cut approval round applies. Uncertain meaning remains a material decision.

Review both sides of joins for words, breath/tails, acoustic discontinuity, gestures, graphic state and captions. Remap footage, original audio, captions, B-roll, graphics, motion and matte together. Check safe areas/faces, dominant words and foreground contours in enlarged critical and consecutive frames. A rectangular face patch is not proof of a correct silhouette. Preserve original color and report capture/export incompatibilities before running an incompatible operation.

Record structure, visual, audio, color and human acceptance separately as pass/fail/unverified with actual observation scope and actionable findings. The CLI tests guarantee that requirements are discoverable and carried into direction; they do not guarantee an agent follows them or establish continuous viewing, listening, HDR fidelity or author acceptance. New mandates change future proposal hashes; existing immutable packets and decisions are not rewritten or promoted.
